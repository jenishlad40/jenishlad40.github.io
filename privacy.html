Privacy Policy

US Citizen Prep · Last updated 22 September 2026

This policy explains what US Citizen Prep collects, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email jenishlad40@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Name and email addressTo create and identify your account
Study progress — questions answered, mock test scores and datesTo show your progress and sync it across your devices
Subscription statusTo unlock Pro features you've paid for
Advertising identifiersOnly if you allow tracking, to show relevant ads on the free plan

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity outside the app.

Microphone and speech

The app can listen when you choose to answer a question out loud. Audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition to turn it into text so your answer can be checked against the official civics answers.

We never record, store, or transmit your audio. The microphone is only active while you are recording an answer, and the resulting text stays on your device except as part of your saved progress.

Who we share data with

  • Google Firebase — stores your account and study progress
  • Google AdMob — serves rewarded ads on the free plan
  • Apple — processes subscription payments; we never see your payment details

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and tracking

On the free plan you can watch an optional ad to unlock an extra question. Before any tracking occurs, iOS asks your permission. If you decline, ads still work — they're just less relevant. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated data from inside the app: Profile → Delete account. This removes your login and your stored progress permanently and cannot be undone.

Subscriptions are billed by Apple and must be cancelled separately in your Apple ID settings. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription.

Children

This app is intended for adults preparing for the U.S. naturalization test and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will be updated. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Not affiliated with USCIS. US Citizen Prep is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services or any government agency. Always confirm current test requirements at uscis.gov/citizenship.
